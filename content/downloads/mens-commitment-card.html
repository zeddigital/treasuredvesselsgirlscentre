Running the session

Facilitator’s sheet · about two hours · print one, copy the cards

Shape of the two hours

15 minWhy we are here. State the test up front: is she safer, freer, and more able to choose than she was before? Everything is measured against it.
30 minFacilitated discussion, in small groups. What do men here actually do when a marriage is proposed for a girl under 18? Let the disagreement happen. It is the useful part.
20 minThe law, plainly. Minimum age 18 (Constitution, Article 31). Informal unions count (Children (Amendment) Act 2016). A sexual act with anyone under 18 is defilement (Penal Code s.129). Most of this is news to most rooms.
20 minPositive parenting under real financial pressure. “Do not marry her off” is not advice unless something replaces it. Discuss what does.
20 minViolence prevention, concretely. What is not acceptable; what to do when another man is doing it; why “it is a family matter” protects the person causing harm.
15 minCommitment cards and referral contacts. Everyone leaves with both.

Watch for these four

  • Decisions transferring to men. An ally removes obstacles; he does not choose destinations. Pay the fee, do not pick the school.
  • Pressure to reconcile. Nobody in this room should be brokering a return to a partner who has caused harm. That is her decision alone.
  • Performance. If involvement is visible to the community but nothing changes in his own household, it is a performance.
  • Men becoming the heroes. Not marrying off a 14-year-old is the floor, not an achievement.

Two rules for the facilitator

Do not collect the cards. A promise made to a visiting organisation is worth much less than one made to a household. Ask each man to name who will hold him to it instead.

Let it be uncomfortable. A session where everyone agrees with everything has not touched anything. Your job is to keep it honest and on time, not to keep it pleasant.
